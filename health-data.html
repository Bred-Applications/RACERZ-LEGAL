Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — Effective 15 July 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to residents of Washington, Nevada, and other US states with consumer-health-data laws, and supplements our Privacy Policy. It describes how RACERZ (Bred Applications, CVR 45915905, Denmark) handles consumer health data.

2. Consumer Health Data We Collect

  • Weight, height, date of birth, gender (provided by you, with consent).
  • Run performance: distance, duration, pace, cadence, step count, elevation, per-kilometre splits.
  • Derived fitness metrics: VDOT fitness score, fatigue profile, personal records, training zones.
  • A mock-location flag used in fitness-data validation.

3. Sources

Directly from you; from your device's sensors during runs you start; and derived by our servers from the above.

4. Why We Collect It

Solely to provide the service you request: run tracking, opponent simulation, fair matchmaking, anti-cheat validation, coaching features, and your own statistics. We collect no consumer health data beyond what these features need.

5. Sharing

Consumer health data is shared only with processors under contract: Supabase (storage) and Google/Firebase (push delivery). RevenueCat (subscriptions) and GitHub (static content) receive no health data. When you race in ranked mode, a derived fitness summary (VDOT score, fatigue profile, recent maximum distance) is shared with your opponent's device solely to simulate your performance — never your health inputs or identity beyond username.

We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share it for advertising. RACERZ contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: full JSON export in the app (Settings), or by email.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app or via the account deletion page — immediate, permanent, and propagated to our processors.
  • Withdraw consent: any time in Settings.
  • No discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

Requests and questions: contact@bredapplications.com. If we refuse a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision; if the appeal fails, you may contact your state Attorney General.

7. Changes and Contact

Material changes are announced with 30 days notice via in-app notification. Contact: contact@bredapplications.com.
