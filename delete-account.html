Delete Your Account

1. In the App (Fastest)

Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

2. Without the App

Email contact@bredapplications.com from the email address tied to your RACERZ account, with the subject "Delete my account". We verify your identity, complete the deletion within 30 days, and send you a confirmation.

What Gets Deleted

Everything tied to your account: profile, runs and statistics, match history, ELO ratings and audit history, posts, comments, likes, friendships, club memberships, chat messages, notifications, and your physiological data. Deletion is complete — nothing is retained.

Active subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google: cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings to stop future charges. Deleting your RACERZ account does not cancel store billing on its own.
